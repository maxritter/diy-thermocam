# DIY-Thermocam Hardware

PCB, enclosure, and Bill of Materials files for DIY-Thermocam, licensed under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](LICENSE): free to build and change for non-commercial use.

- **[PCB (`pcb/`)](pcb/README.md)** — KiCad 10 sources for the V4.0 main board (fully assembled by the fab), with Gerbers, BOM/CPL exports and 3D STEP models (`pcb/fab/`).
- **[Enclosure (`enclosure/`)](enclosure/README.md)** — Parametric [build123d](https://github.com/gumyr/build123d) CAD (`enclosure/cad/`), 3D-printed and laser-cut exports (`enclosure/exports/`), and Blender renders (`enclosure/renders/`).
- **[Bill of Materials (`bom/`)](bom/README.md)** — Single-source `bom.csv`, schema validator, and order guide.
