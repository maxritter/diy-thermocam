## Ordering & Fabrication Notes

- **FLIR Lepton sensor:** **Lepton 3.5** (`160×120`, 57° HFOV, recommended), optional wide-angle **Lepton 3.1R** (`160×120`, 95° HFOV / 119° DFOV, `500-0758-03`), and **Lepton 2.5** (`80×60`, EOL notice Sept 2027) all fit the **Lepton Breakout Board V2.0**. Both V3.0 and V4.0 parametric enclosures clear the full 119° cone of Lepton 3.1R. Do not substitute PureThermal USB UVC boards — they lack the 2×10 SPI header.
- **PCB & Enclosure fabrication:** Order bare boards or V4.0 turnkey PCBA (31 verified LCSC SKUs) from **JLCPCB**, **Seeed Studio Fusion**, or **PCBWay** using the Gerber, Excellon, BOM, and CPL exports in [`hardware/pcb/`](https://github.com/maxritter/diy-thermocam/tree/master/hardware/pcb). 3D-print / CNC the shell or laser-cut 3 mm acrylic from [`hardware/enclosure/`](https://github.com/maxritter/diy-thermocam/tree/master/hardware/enclosure).
- **Display options (BuyDisplay `ER-TFTM032-3`):** Select **Pin Header 4-Wire SPI**, **5V Power Supply**, **3.2-inch Resistive Touch Panel**, and **No Font Chip**.
- **Battery & polarity:** V4.0's 3D battery cradle requires a slim **≤5.0 mm UL/IEC 62133 protected `505060` (5.0 × 50 × 60 mm) 2000 mAh LiPo** wired to standard Adafruit/SparkFun JST-PH polarity (always verify `+`/`-` against `J4` silkscreen). V4.0 adds an active `AO3401A`/`AO3400A` reverse-polarity MOSFET interlock + 2 A PTC fuse, though you should still check polarity before plugging in.
