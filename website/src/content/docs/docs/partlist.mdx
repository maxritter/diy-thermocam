## Ordering notes

- **FLIR Lepton sensor:** Lepton 3.5 (`160×120`, 57° HFOV), wide-angle Lepton 3.1R (`160×120`, 95° HFOV / 119° DFOV, `500-0758-03`), and Lepton 2.5 (`80×60`, EOL notice Sept 2027) all fit Lepton Breakout Board V2.0. Do not substitute a PureThermal USB board — it lacks the 2×10 SPI header.
- **PCB and enclosure fabrication:** Upload the Gerbers, BOM, and CPL from [`hardware/pcb/fab/`](https://github.com/maxritter/diy-thermocam/tree/master/hardware/pcb/fab) to JLCPCB, Seeed Studio Fusion, or PCBWay. Enclosure exports live in [`hardware/enclosure/exports/`](https://github.com/maxritter/diy-thermocam/tree/master/hardware/enclosure/exports).
- **Display (BuyDisplay `ER-TFTM032-3`):** Choose 4-Wire SPI, 5V power supply, 3.2-inch resistive touch, pin header, and no font chip.
- **Battery (V4.0 3D cradle):** Use a slim ≤5.0 mm UL/IEC 62133 protected `505060` 2000 mAh LiPo with Adafruit/SparkFun JST-PH polarity. Always verify `+`/`-` against the `J4` silkscreen.
